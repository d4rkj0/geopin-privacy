First Player

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the app First Player, developed by Pin Soft House - Giuseppe Maiello, handles user data.

Effective Date

Last updated: 04/27/2026

Data Collection

First Player does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

The app does not require users to create an account and does not ask for personal information such as name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or payment information.

App Functionality

First Player is a simple utility app designed to randomly choose the first player for board games, card games, party games, or similar group activities.

Players place their fingers on the screen, and the app randomly selects who starts the game. All interactions happen locally on the device.

Permissions

First Player may use basic device features only to support the app experience, such as touch input and, if enabled, device vibration for feedback.

These features are not used to identify users and are not used to collect personal information.

Internet and Third-Party Services

First Player does not require an internet connection to work.

The app does not use analytics services, advertising networks, tracking tools, external SDKs, or third-party data collection services.

Data Sharing

Since First Player does not collect personal data, no personal data is shared with third parties.

Data Retention

First Player does not store personal data.

Because no personal data is collected or stored, there is no personal data to retain or delete.

Children's Privacy

First Player does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

The app is intended as a simple game utility and does not include features designed to collect information from users of any age.

Security

Since First Player does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no personal user data stored on external servers.
